ABOUT AFGHANISTAN

A Land of History, Culture and Natural Beauty

Afghanistan is a country in Central and South Asia known for its historic cities, traditional culture, beautiful valleys, and spectacular mountains.

From the historic streets of Herat to the blue lakes of Band-e Amir in Bamyan, every region offers a different experience.
